## Section 4 — Why Indi for a group specifically

*The differentiator section, the persuasion core. Why us over an in-house hire or a generalist agency.*

> **Eyebrow:** Why Indi
> **Heading:** The team you'd build, without building it.

Three points:
> - **Cheaper than in-house, deeper than an agency.** A full commercial team is a major payroll commitment. A generalist agency doesn't know hotels. Indi is a specialist hotel commercial team you can plug in immediately, and scale as the portfolio grows.
